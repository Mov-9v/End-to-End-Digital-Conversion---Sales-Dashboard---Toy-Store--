- Measure Name: Funnel 2 - Products Page

  Description: Total sessions navigating to the central product listing catalog[cite: 1]. Evaluates landing page clarity and entry-level browsing motivation.

- Measure Name: Funnel 3 - Product Details
  Baseline Value: 210,214
  Unit: Integer (Count)
  Description: Total unique sessions reaching an individual Product Detail Page (PDP). Distinguishes general browsing from concrete product exploration.

- Measure Name: Funnel 4 - Cart
  Baseline Value: 94,953[cite: 1]
  Unit: Integer (Count)
  Description: Total sessions advancing to the shopping cart[cite: 1]. Primary checkpoint for commercial intent prior to checkout initiation.

- Measure Name: Funnel 5 - Shipping
  Baseline Value: 64,484[cite: 1]
  Unit: Integer (Count)
  Description: Total sessions entering the shipping detail step[cite: 1]. Measures drop-off between cart addition and active checkout commitment.

- Measure Name: Funnel 6 - Billing
  Baseline Value: 52,058
  Unit: Integer (Count)
  Description: Total sessions passing shipping validation and loading the payment gateway. Diagnoses checkout friction caused by shipping costs or carrier rules.

- Measure Name: Funnel 7 - Order Completed
  Baseline Value: 32,313[cite: 1]
  Unit: Integer (Count)
  Description: Completed orders landing on the order confirmation screen[cite: 1]. Serves as the ground-truth transaction volume verified against backend database records[cite: 1].


[Step Progression Rates - Micro Conversion]

- Measure Name: CR - Entry to Products
  Baseline Value: 55.24%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Progression rate from initial landing pages to the product catalog. Validates Hypothesis 1 by disproving ad-to-landing-page misalignment.

- Measure Name: CR - Products to Details
  Baseline Value: 80.47%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Progression rate from the catalog view to individual product pages. Confirms strong catalog engagement and layout navigation efficiency.

- Measure Name: CR - Details to Cart
  Baseline Value: 45.17%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Progression rate from PDP views to cart additions. Identifies the primary conversion bottleneck (~55% drop-off), validating the core product-page friction hypothesis.

- Measure Name: CR - Cart to Shipping
  Baseline Value: 67.91%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Progression rate from cart review into the shipping funnel. Quantifies cart abandonment and checkout-initiation friction.

- Measure Name: CR - Shipping to Billing
  Baseline Value: 80.73%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Progression rate across the shipping detail screen. Proves shipping address submission is not a major drop-off driver.

- Measure Name: CR - Billing to Order
  Baseline Value: 62.07%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Final payment gateway conversion rate. Measures friction related to available payment methods, form fields, and gateway validation errors.

- Measure Name: CR - Entry to Cart
  Baseline Value: 20.08%
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Macro conversion rate covering the entire pre-checkout journey. Evaluates upstream UX independent of the payment pipeline.

- Measure Name: Overall Store CR
  Baseline Value: 6.83%[cite: 1]
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: End-to-end sitewide conversion rate (Orders / Sessions)[cite: 1]. Benchmark reference used to anchor executive KPI cards against market averages[cite: 1].

  Description: Gross operational volume of all physical units sold across primary and add-on transactions[cite: 1]. Baseline denominator for defect rate calculations.

  Baseline Value: 1,731[cite: 1]
  Unit: Integer (Count)
  Description: Total returned or refunded units recorded in post-purchase logs[cite: 1]. Tracks customer dissatisfaction and operational return volume.

- Measure Name: Store Refund Rate
  Baseline Value: 4.32%[cite: 1]
  Unit: Percentage
  Description: Overall store return rate (Refunded Units / Total Sold)[cite: 1]. Disproves a systemic or catalog-wide quality failure by falling well within healthy e-commerce benchmarks.
